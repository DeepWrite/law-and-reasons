# Historical Issue: 2025 - Authority, Reasons, and Legal Content after Hart

This is the source marker for the temporary public model issue. The rendered publication copy is maintained in `site/_historical_issues/2025.md` so that GitHub Pages can publish it through the Jekyll historical issue collection.

The model issue demonstrates the standing editorial depth policy:

- orthodox legal philosophy and legal theory remain the center of gravity;
- representative top-tier journal coverage is required;
- canonical legal philosophers supply the argumentative frame;
- AI and computational topics are included only when they bear directly on legal-philosophical questions such as authority, reasons, authorship, interpretation, institutional responsibility, and legal content;
- access levels and uncertainty notes remain visible.

It also demonstrates the section-level editorial-agent rule. A public historical model issue must record which editorial role is responsible for the source register, historical editor's note, field map, journal/book coverage, ten-work bibliography, debate map, concepts under pressure, retrospective note, teaching note, and final editorial note. The public copy stores that map in front matter and renders it in an editorial agent register.
