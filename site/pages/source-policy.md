---
title: "Historical Source Policy"
subtitle: "Access levels, analysis levels, and private access reviews"
permalink: /source-policy/
---

Do not pretend to have read historical works when only bibliographic metadata is available. Do not infer detailed arguments from titles alone. Use book reviews and later literature cautiously.

Every article or chapter must record its public access level and analysis level. Public files must not disclose whether the Chief Editor or any private library holds the full text of a specific work. Use `private_access_status` where non-public access information exists, and keep actual holdings or acquisition status outside the public repository.
