Law and Reasons: A Review of Legal Philosophy and Legal Theory

법과 이유 / Law and Reasons is a bilingual review of legal philosophy and legal theory.

The magazine has a contemporary quarterly review and a historical back-issue program. Historical issues do not publish simple hindsight summaries; each issue keeps time-situated analysis, retrospective notes, and archival uncertainty separate.
